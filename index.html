The problem

Sports research is scattered

If you play fantasy or follow player props, you usually jump between five or six sites: one for stats, one for injuries, one for lines, one for matchups. Most of them hand you numbers without telling you what they mean. ProScope puts it in one place and explains it.

What it does

Everything you check before a game

Coverage: the NFL and NBA have everything below. College football has game breakdowns, featured players, props and the Top 25. College basketball has game breakdowns and the Top 25, with player props coming midseason.

Game breakdowns

Each matchup is written up like an analyst would talk you through it: who's hot, where the mismatch is, who's missing, and ProScope's pick.

Player projections

What each player should do tonight, plus a realistic range, so you see the typical game and the ceiling.

Prop breakdowns

Pick a stat and tap through the sportsbook's main and alternate lines. A chart shows his last 10 games against each line, with how often he's cleared it.

ProScope Picks

A ranked top 10 of ProScope's favorite props each day, for each sport and all sports combined, with the reasons and his last 10 games against the line. Tap any name to open his full page.

Live games

Live scores, a full box score, and the win chance updating as the game goes.

Injuries that matter

Who's out, what role they play (top scorer, top target, starting QB), and who picks up the work.

Fantasy rankings

Weekly NFL and daily NBA rankings with start or sit calls and waiver targets.

College Top 25

The AP poll next to ProScope's own Top 25, built from who teams beat and lost to.

How it works

From raw data to a plain-English read

1 · Collect

Pull the data

Box scores, play-by-play, official injury reports, weather and sportsbook lines.

2 · Test

Prove it first

Every model is checked against past seasons. If an idea doesn't make predictions better, it doesn't go on the site.

3 · Explain

Write it up

Numbers become breakdowns that lead with each game's biggest storyline.

4 · Update

Keep it fresh

Lines, picks and snapshots refresh every hour on game days, with no one clicking.

What makes it different

Tested, explained, all in one place

  • Tested before it shipsEvery projection and model is checked against past seasons before it goes live. Projection ranges are built so about 8 in 10 games land inside them, and they're re-checked every week.
  • Ideas get cutPopular angles like revenge games, division rivalries, hot streaks and a defense missing its top cornerback were tested on years of games and left out where they didn't make the projections better.
  • Written like a personEvery breakdown reads like someone who knows the sport explaining it, and each one opens with that game's own storyline.
  • Graded every weekEvery pick is saved before the game and graded afterward, and the models are adjusted from the results.
  • Free for early usersNo account and no paywall. Open the site and start researching.
